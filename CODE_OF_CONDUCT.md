# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in Stash a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior

* **Respectful communication** — disagree technically, not personally
* **Constructive feedback** — focus on code, design, ideas; not people
* **Inclusivity** — welcome newcomers, use inclusive language, respect pronouns
* **Collaboration** — share knowledge, mentor when asked, credit others' work
* **Professionalism** — stay on topic, keep discussions productive
* **Empathy** — consider how your words affect others

### Unacceptable Behavior

* Harassment, insults, or demeaning language
* Trolling, baiting, or deliberate disruption
* Personal attacks, doxxing, or threats
* Discriminatory jokes, slurs, or microaggressions
* Sexualized language or advances
* Publishing private information without consent
* Spam, self-promotion, or off-topic vendor advocacy
* Any conduct inappropriate in a professional open-source setting

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces:

* GitHub repositories (issues, PRs, discussions, wiki)
* Project communication channels (Discord, Matrix, mailing lists, etc.)
* Project-related events (virtual or in-person)
* Any interaction where you represent the project

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:

* Clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior
* Taking appropriate corrective action for violations
* Removing, editing, or rejecting contributions that violate this Code
* Communicating reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code in good faith may face temporary or permanent consequences determined by project leadership.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to:

**[project maintainers / security contact]**

All reports are reviewed promptly and confidentially. The reporting party's identity is protected.

Include:

* Description of the incident
* Link(s) to relevant discussion (if public)
* Names/usernames involved
* Any additional context

---

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will follow these community impact guidelines:

| Impact | Consequence |
|--------|-------------|
| **Correction** — minor, first-time, unintentional | Private written warning; explanation of impact |
| **Warning** — repeated or moderately harmful | Temporary restriction from project spaces (e.g., 7 days) |
| **Temporary Ban** — serious or persistent harm | Temporary ban from all project spaces (e.g., 30–90 days) |
| **Permanent Ban** — severe, repeated, or deliberate harm | Permanent removal from all project spaces |

Decisions consider context, intent, pattern, and impact. Repeat offenses escalate consequences.
